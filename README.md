# 👋 Welcome to My WiFi Pineapple Pager Projects

Hi, I'm **spywill**!

Welcome to my collection of projects for the **Hak5 WiFi Pineapple Pager**. This repository is where I share my work, experiments, ideas, and custom payloads as I continue learning, developing, and exploring what the Pager can do.

## 🛠️ About Me

I enjoy working with Bash scripting, DuckyScript, and Linux, especially when it comes to creating useful tools and custom payloads for Hak5 gear.

A lot of my work involves experimenting with ideas, writing scripts, building menus, improving existing projects, troubleshooting problems, and finding new ways to make the most of the WiFi Pineapple Pager.

I enjoy the process of taking an idea and turning it into something that actually works on the device. Not everything works perfectly on the first try, but that's part of the fun — learning, testing, fixing things, and making improvements along the way.

## 🍍 Why Hak5?

A big thank-you to **[Hak5](https://hak5.org/)** for creating such a great platform and the gear that makes projects like these possible.

I enjoy working with Hak5 devices because they give me the opportunity to experiment, learn new skills, and develop my own tools. The WiFi Pineapple Pager is a great device to explore scripting, automation, and Linux-based development in a portable format.

I appreciate the work that goes into making these platforms available to the community, and I'm happy to contribute my own projects and ideas.

## 💻 What You'll Find Here

This repository is a collection of projects I've developed and continue to improve for the WiFi Pineapple Pager, including:

- **Custom Payloads** — Scripts and tools designed to add useful functionality to the Pager.
- **Bash & DuckyScript** — Experiments, automation, menus, and scripting projects.
- **Utilities & Tools** — Small projects that make everyday tasks easier or more interesting.
- **Display & UI Projects** — Custom menus, visual effects, and ways to experiment with the Pager's interface.
- **Ongoing Experiments** — New ideas, improvements, fixes, and projects that grow as I learn more.

Each project is an opportunity to learn something new, solve a problem, and share what I've discovered with others.
