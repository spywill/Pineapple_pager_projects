# Fake ME

A fictional identity generator for the Hak5 WiFi Pineapple Pager. Generate random US and Canadian profiles with realistic-looking personal, employment, family, financial, and identification details.

## Features

- **Country Selection:** Choose USA or Canada.
- **Gender Selection:** Choose Male or Female.
- **Personal Information:** Random names, age, date of birth, and physical characteristics.
- **Address & Contact:** Street address, phone number, email, and mobile carrier.
- **Education:** Random high schools and colleges based on state or province.
- **Family Information:** Parents, spouse, children, siblings, and marital status.
- **Employment:** Company, job title, department, salary, employee ID, and employment status.
- **Personal Interests:** Hobbies, music, sports, food, movies, and travel destinations.
- **Identification:** Fictional SSN/SIN, driver license, passport, and health card information.
- **Financial Details:** Generated payment card and bank account information.
- **Vehicle Information:** Random vehicle make, model, year, color, and license plate.

## Requirements

- Hak5 WiFi Pineapple Pager
- Bash
- Pager UI functions (`LIST_PICKER`, `LOG`)

## Usage

1. Launch **Fake ME**.
2. Select USA or Canada.
3. Choose Male or Female.
4. View the generated fictional identity in the Pager display.

## Disclaimer

This payload generates fictional identity information for testing, development, and educational purposes only.

All generated identities, identification numbers, financial details, and personal information are randomly generated and are not valid credentials or proof of identity. Do not use generated information for fraud, impersonation, or illegal activities.
